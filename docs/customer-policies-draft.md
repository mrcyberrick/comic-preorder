## 1. Pull-List Policy — Ray & Judy's Book Stop

*Effective [CONFIRM date]*

### How it works
Each month we load the new catalogs from our distributors. You reserve what you want, and we order each copy specifically for you. Most titles arrive one to three months after you reserve them.

### Order deadline
The Catalog page shows the month's **order deadline**. You can add, change or remove reservations freely until then.

### After we order
Once a title shows **Order placed** on My List, it has been ordered from the distributor for you and can no longer be cancelled. Please make sure you want something before the deadline.

### Subscriptions
Subscribing to a series reserves the standard cover of each new issue automatically every month. Variant covers are not included; reserve those separately. You can unsubscribe at any time; issues already ordered for you stay on your list.

### Pricing and discount
Pull-list customers receive **10% off every pre-order** reserved through PULLLIST. Prices shown are the publisher's cover price; the discount is applied when you pay. Publishers occasionally change prices; you pay the price on the book when it arrives, less your discount.

### Payment
Nothing is charged online. **Payment is due when you pick up.** We do not take deposits.

### Pickup
When your books arrive they appear on **This Week**, and we hold them for you. Please pick up within **six weeks** of arrival. Books not collected after six weeks may be returned to the shelf for sale. [CONFIRM: anything else after six weeks, e.g. pausing the account after repeated missed pickups?]

### No minimum
There is no minimum number of titles to keep a pull list.

### Items we can't guarantee
- **Variant and incentive covers** (for example 1:25 or 1:50 ratio covers) depend on what the distributor allocates to us. These are marked in the catalog, and we can't promise them.
- **Supplier cancellations and shortages.** Sometimes a publisher cancels or delays a title, or the distributor ships short. If that happens, My List shows it under *No longer coming*. You're never charged for a book you don't receive.
- **Damaged copies.** If a copy arrives damaged, we handle it with the supplier; you don't need to call.

### Questions
Call us at 973-586-9182 or stop in at the shop in Rockaway, NJ.

## 3. Privacy Statement

*Effective [CONFIRM date]*

This statement explains what personal information PULLLIST collects, why, and what we do with it.

### What we collect
- **Account details:** your name, email address, and phone number if you give one. Your password is stored only in encrypted (hashed) form; nobody, including shop staff, can read it.
- **Your pull list:** your reservations, subscriptions, and a history of past reservations.
- **Activity on the site:** simple records of actions such as visiting, reserving or cancelling, used to improve the service and help the shop understand demand. These are deleted after **90 days**.
- **Browser storage:** the site stores your sign-in session and a few display preferences in your browser. We don't use advertising cookies or third-party ad trackers.

### How we use it
- To run your pull list: record your reservations, place your shop's orders, and show you what has arrived.
- To send account emails, such as sign-up confirmation, approval, password resets, and your list when you ask for it.
- To keep the service secure and working.

### Who can see it
- **Your shop's staff** can see your account details and pull list, and can manage them on your behalf.
- **Service providers** that run PULLLIST for us, under their own data-protection terms:
  - **Supabase:** database and sign-in
  - **Cloudflare:** website hosting and the bot check on the sign-up form
  - **Resend:** account emails
- **Distributors and publishers do not receive your personal information.** Orders to them list items and quantities only.

We **do not sell** your personal information or share it for advertising.

### Newsletter
The shop's weekly newsletter is a separate sign-up with its own unsubscribe link. Having a PULLLIST account doesn't sign you up for it. [CONFIRM]

### How long we keep it
We keep your account and pull list while your account is open. If your account is closed, your activity records are anonymised; the shop may keep basic order records it needs for its own bookkeeping. [CONFIRM]

### Your choices
You can view your reservations and subscriptions at any time, and update your name and phone through your shop. To have your account and personal information deleted, contact your shop or email us.

### Children
PULLLIST is not intended for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

We may update this statement. The effective date at the top shows the latest version.
